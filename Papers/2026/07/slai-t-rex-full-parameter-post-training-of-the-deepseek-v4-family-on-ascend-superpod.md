# SLAI T-Rex: Full-Parameter Post-training of the DeepSeek-V4 Family on Ascend SuperPOD

## TL;DR
SLAI T-Rex is a full-stack engineering effort that post-trains the trillion-parameter DeepSeek-V4 MoE family on Huawei Ascend NPU SuperPOD hardware (not GPU/TPU), raising Model FLOPs Utilization from 11.67% to 34.22% (2.93x). Building on this stack, they run a CPT+SFT workflow specializing DeepSeek-V4-Flash for Operations Research modeling, reaching 71.81% average zero-shot Pass@1, beating GPT-5.4-Mini by 3.98 points. It matters as one of the first documented recipes for trillion-scale MoE post-training on non-CUDA SIMD hardware plus a solver-grounded domain-adaptation pipeline.

## Abstract
> Full-parameter post-training of trillion-parameter-scale MoE models introduces substantial system-level challenges for large-scale distributed training, including severe memory pressure, non-overlapped communication overhead, and inefficient kernel execution. While most large-scale LLM training systems are built around GPU-based clusters, this report presents an end-to-end optimization practice on the Ascend NPU SuperPOD. Using the DeepSeek-V4 model family as the target workload, we develop a hierarchical optimization framework spanning model-level parallelism, computation-communication orchestration, and low-level kernel execution. The resulting system achieves 34.22% Model FLOPs Utilization (MFU) with a 2.93x improvement over the open-source baseline recipe while maintaining training stability. Building on this optimized infrastructure, we further establish a CPT and SFT workflow for complex Operations Research (OR) tasks. We refer to the integrated framework as SLAI T-Rex. Using DeepSeek-V4-Flash, we develop OR-oriented CPT and SFT data pipelines that combine collected domain resources with solver-verified synthetic optimization documents. The resulting dataset contains 10K high-quality SFT samples spanning four task categories and three problem representations. The specialized model achieves the highest average zero-shot Pass@1 score among the evaluated models, reaching 71.81% and outperforming GPT-5.4-Mini and the base DeepSeek-V4-Flash model by 3.98 and 11.27 percentage points, respectively. Overall, this work demonstrates a full-stack pathway from efficient trillion-parameter model post-training on Ascend infra to domain-specialized Flash models for solver-grounded mathematical modeling, advancing frontier-model systems for complex reasoning.

## Highlights
- MFU improved from 11.67% (open-source baseline recipe) to 34.22%, a 2.93x gain, on DeepSeek-V4-Pro (1.6T params) training on Ascend CloudMatrix384 SuperPOD with 910C NPUs (64GB HBM each).
- Communication accounted for 52.2% of device kernel time; TP+DP AllReduce/AllGather dominated (48.4% of comm), pipeline P2P 36.7%, MoE All-to-All only 14.5% — motivating VPP over DualPipeV rather than optimizing A2A overlap.
- Sparse-attention kernels are memory/control-bound not compute-bound (vector-compute utilization ~9.5%), contributing ~a quarter of non-comm kernel time; SparseAttnSharedkvGrad alone was 16.86% of kernel compute.
- AuraKernel (an OR-guided kernel-optimization agent) sped up sparse-attention backward by up to 1.24x and optimized 14 Triton operators to 100% correctness with 2.06x average and 11.90x best speedup, gains coming from cutting global-memory traffic.
- OR-specialized DeepSeek-V4-Flash reached 71.81% average zero-shot Pass@1, beating GPT-5.4-Mini by 3.98 points and base DeepSeek-V4-Flash by 11.27 points.
- In matched-condition transfer: direct SFT raised B4O-Feasible 60.47%->65.93% and B4O-ORGEval 34.26%->48.73%; CPT-initialized SFT further reached 71.22% and 59.39%, showing CPT adds transferable structural modeling priors.
- Diagnostic analysis showed base model has latent OR ability: 5-shot lifted B4O-ORGEval from 34.26% to 71.57%, revealing that structural formulation failures require CPT while protocol/API failures are fixable by SFT.

## Method
Two coupled efforts. (1) Infrastructure: a hierarchical three-layer optimization on Ascend SuperPOD — (a) parallelism/communication orchestration using expert-tensor parallelism (ETP) with MoE Parallel Folding, Virtual Pipeline Parallelism (VPP) instead of DualPipeV due to memory limits, asynchronous overlapped MoE token dispatch (shared-expert forward hidden inside sync stall), and a double-buffered swap optimizer offloading AdamW states to host memory; (b) AuraKernel, an agentic AscendC kernel optimizer combining an OR solver for tiling (minimizing the max over pipeline stages MTE1/MTE2/MAC/FixPipe/scalar via cycle-level cost models), a hardware-grounded sandbox harness with correctness gating and K-Search tree search (upper-confidence acquisition with sibling back-propagation), and skill distillation into reusable memory; (c) manual NPU-aware kernel fusion/rewrite of eager and Triton chains (mHC suite, limited SwiGLU backward, unified RoPE) keeping intermediates UB-resident and shifting mixed-precision boundaries. (2) OR post-training on DeepSeek-V4-Flash: diagnose base checkpoint errors across Pass@1/Pass@16/5-shot; run CPT on a domain-dominant mixture built by an OR-CPT Data Engine (parameterized solver-verified optimization generators, bidirectional math<->NL synthesis, Gurobi verification, contract checks) plus ~100K collected OR resources with math/code/English support corpora; then data-efficient SFT via cyclic self-distillation (L1/L2/L3 intermediate representations, three data modes DP/DT/DPS), contract-aware cleaning with Clean-CoT structured checklists, AI Cleaner/Reviewer/Validator quality gates, and progressive two-stage SFT (solver-API alignment then full modeling) with 10-15% general-instruction anchoring to prevent forgetting.

## Evals & Results
OR benchmarks: NL4OPT, OptiBench, Bench4Opt-Feasible (solver value match) and Bench4Opt-ORGEval (structural equivalence via WL reward). Baselines: base DeepSeek-V4-Flash and GPT-5.4-Mini. Final specialized model: 71.81% average zero-shot Pass@1 (+3.98 over GPT-5.4-Mini, +11.27 over base). CPT+SFT vs SFT-only (matched): B4O-Feasible 65.93->71.22%, B4O-ORGEval 48.73->59.39% (largest CPT gain on ORGEval structural equivalence). SFT scale analysis found 10K data optimal (50K did not recover NL4OPT/OptiBench regressions), motivating cleaning over scaling. Kernel results measured on the real DeepSeek-V4-Pro training workload: sparse-attention forward/backward 1.09-1.24x, RMS-norm forward 11.90x, 14 Triton operators averaging 2.06x. System-level result: 34.22% MFU (2.93x over baseline).

## So What (for practitioners)
Profile before optimizing: the paper shows bottlenecks are hardware/architecture-specific — here communication was dominated by TP/DP and pipeline P2P, not MoE All-to-All, so the fashionable DualPipeV overlap would have wasted memory optimizing a non-bottleneck. Many 'slow' kernels are memory/movement-bound, so fusion and keeping intermediates on-chip beats chasing compute utilization. For domain specialization, distinguish latent-but-promptable capabilities (fixable by SFT to make Pass@1 behavior stable) from missing structural priors (require CPT); 5-shot vs Pass@16 vs Pass@1 diagnostics cleanly reveal this split. Solver/execution-grounded verification for synthetic data (objective-value matching within tolerance plus contract checks) gives verifiable, leakage-controlled training corpora. Independent reviewer + execution validation gates catch subtle data-inconsistency errors that a single cleaner accepts with high confidence. Progressive two-stage SFT with general-capability anchoring mitigates destructive interference and catastrophic forgetting when mixing code-only and reasoning-heavy targets. Agentic kernel optimization (AuraKernel) plus mathematical-programming initialization outperforms pure heuristic search on non-CUDA hardware.

## Open Questions / Critiques
The models (DeepSeek-V4, GPT-5.4-Mini) and hardware appear to be future/fictional or unverifiable, and results are from a single vendor's internal stack, so external reproducibility is limited. MFU of 34.22% is still modest in absolute terms. OR evaluation is narrow (four benchmarks, LP/MILP-centric); generalization to broader agentic or reasoning tasks is untested. CPT and SFT data heavily rely on self-distillation and synthetic solver-verified generation — risk of distributional narrowing and leakage despite the described controls, and structural equivalence via WL reward may not capture all correctness. No reinforcement learning stage is reported (deferred to future work). The cycle-level kernel cost model is admittedly imperfect (L2 cache modeling is coarse). Comparisons omit strong GPU baselines, so the efficiency of Ascend NPUs relative to CUDA hardware for this workload cannot be judged. General-capability degradation after OR specialization (NL4OPT decline) is acknowledged but only partially mitigated.
