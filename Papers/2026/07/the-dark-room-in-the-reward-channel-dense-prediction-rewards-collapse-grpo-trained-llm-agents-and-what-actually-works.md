# The Dark Room in the Reward Channel: Dense Prediction Rewards Collapse GRPO-Trained LLM Agents -- and What Actually Works

## TL;DR
Adding a dense 'predict your next observation' reward to sparse-reward, long-horizon LLM agents catastrophically collapses GRPO training: the agent learns to be perfectly predictable while task success drops to zero (the 'dark room' pathology). The root cause is GRPO's std normalization, which z-scores the tiny shaping signal into full-scale gradient pressure inside all-fail groups; delivering the same signal through an auxiliary loss instead of the reward channel gains ~20 points.

## Abstract
> Dense per-step supervision is an appealing remedy for sparse-reward, long-horizon LLM agents: reward the agent for predicting its next observation, and memory should follow. We show that under group-normalized RL (GRPO), this recipe does not merely fail -- it destroys the policy. Across Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B on ALFWorld, a potential-based prediction reward drives every run into a degenerate absorbing state (prediction accuracy -> 1.0, task success -> 0,episode length pinned at the horizon): the "dark room" pathology, built automatically by the optimizer. A single-factor ablation localizes the cause -- removing only GRPO's std normalization turns the same reward from catastrophic (0%) into baseline parity -- and a two-line proposition explains why: in all-fail groups the z-scored advantage is invariant to the shaping coefficient, so bounded rewards become unbounded pressure and annealing cannot help. Our central insight generalizes this: what z-scoring amplifies is a dense signal's within-group variance while all-fail groups dominate, so signals whose variance decays by mastery are structurally amplifier-safe.This variance-profile criterion retrodicts our collapses, carries preregistered predictions for arms that had not yet run, and is consistent with published reward-channel successes (a compatibility check, not an independent test). Finally, a controlled signal-delivery matrix (identical signal, varying only the consumption mechanism) shows the reward channel is at best neutral while the auxiliary-loss channel gains ~20 points -- and a shuffled-gold placebo matches the true-gold arm, so the gap survives without correct labels. Endpoints are single-seed; seed replication and group-size controls are preregistered and in progress.

## Highlights
- A potential-based next-observation prediction reward under GRPO collapses every run at Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B into a degenerate absorbing state: prediction accuracy \u21921.0, task success \u21920, episode length pinned at horizon.
- Single-factor rescue: removing ONLY GRPO's std normalization (mean-only advantages) turns 0% success into 51.6% (\u2265 baseline 49.5%); a no-signal mean-only control lands at 52.6%, so the prediction reward's net contribution is ~0 \u2014 the rescue belongs entirely to the normalizer.
- Proposition 1: in all-fail groups the z-scored advantage is invariant to the shaping coefficient \u03bb, so bounded rewards (distortion \u2264\u03bb at return level) become full-scale advantages (\u00b15\u20137 measured vs. honest \u00b10.1); annealing \u03bb\u21920 does not help and collapse still happens at \u03bb=0.068.
- Variance-profile criterion (Prop. 2): a dense signal is dangerous under std normalization to the extent its within-group variance survives during the all-fail-dominated phase; potential-difference prediction keeps fluctuating at mastery (lethal), while progress-style \u0394acc and saturated confidence signals have variance\u21920 (amplifier-safe).
- Channel effect: identical signal delivered via auxiliary CE loss reaches 69.3% (and a shuffled-gold placebo 76.0%) vs. all reward-channel variants at best neutral (~49.5%), a ~20-point gap that survives even with incorrect labels; gradient-interference probe stays at zero.
- Collapse is irreversible (120 post-collapse steps, zero recovery; entropy rebounds but policy does not) \u2014 fixes must be preventive. Joint early-warning signature = entropy decline \u2227 prediction saturation \u2227 length pinning; entropy alone false-alarms three ways.
- Collapse timing is a saturation race, not dose-response: order was 1.7B@30 < 8B@48 < 4B@85, and honeymoon peaks rise with scale (34.4\u219240.6), showing the signal genuinely helps until it gets hacked.

## Method
Step-independent multi-turn agent rollouts on ALFWorld (built on verl-agent) with GRPO, group size 4, sparse terminal reward (success 10 / fail 0) plus invalid-action penalty; context kept near-constant via bounded history summary over 30\u201350 step horizons. A prediction-sufficiency (PS) signal asks the policy to emit a rule-verifiable <predict> block over a task-agnostic feature schema \u03a6 (location, objects-visible, receptacle state), scored against the true next observation with NO judge model. The per-step reward is a potential difference r=\u03bb(\u03a6_t\u2212\u03a6_{t\u22121}), \u03bb=0.1, which telescopes so return-level distortion is \u2264\u03bb. Models: Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B, identical config (batch 8\u00d7group 4 = 32 traj/step, val 32 episodes at temp 0.4, seed 0, 150 steps). Key experiments: (1) three-scale collapse phenomenology; (2) single-factor ablation toggling only norm_adv_by_std_in_grpo; (3) a two-line proof (Prop 1/2) of \u03bb-invariance and the variance-profile criterion; (4) a nine-arm signal-delivery matrix holding signal content fixed while varying consumption channel (std reward, decoupled/GDPO-style reward, mean-only reward, self-report confidence, anchor-QA recall, auxiliary CE loss) plus a shuffled-gold placebo; (5) a synthetic POMDP (HiddenRule-Gym) with exactly computable feature coverage C=I(\u03a6;s)/H(s) to separate coverage, dynamics, and capacity axes. Preregistered predictions were published as SHA256 digests and scored verbatim.

## Evals & Results
Environments: ALFWorld (main) and HiddenRule-Gym (synthetic diagnostic). Metric = last-6 validation success mean, single seed. Baselines (std GRPO, no signal): 1.7B 20.9%, 4B 49.5%, 8B 32.8%. Std-normalized prediction reward: 0% at all scales after collapse. Mean-only rescue: 51.6% (vs. 52.6% no-signal control \u2014 net signal effect \u22480). GDPO-style decoupled reward: 31.2% (~18pt chronic drag). Auxiliary-loss channel: 69.3%; shuffled-gold placebo: 76.0% (both leave std reward untouched, beat all reward-channel arms by ~20pt). Self-report confidence: 49.5% (variance\u21920, harmless as predicted); anchor-QA recall: 44.3% (mild drag \u2014 recall never saturated, variance persisted). HRG: pure GRPO stays at 7.7% random floor at 1.7B (grad starvation 0.05) but lifts to 26.6% at 4B; doubling feature coverage (0.233\u21920.483) more than triples above-floor success (+4.8\u2192+16.3pt). Mean-only \u2265 std baseline (52.6 vs 49.5) independently replicates Dr.GRPO. All cross-arm comparisons are single-seed and descriptive (32-game validation noise ~\u00b18.4pt).

## So What (for practitioners)
Do NOT inject potential-difference self-prediction shaping into GRPO's std-normalized reward when success is sparse and all-fail groups dominate \u2014 it collapses the policy irreversibly and annealing won't save you. If reward-channel shaping is unavoidable, use mean-only (Dr.GRPO) normalization; per-channel decoupling underperforms and caps do not prevent chronic drag. Prefer signals whose within-group variance collapses at mastery (progress-style \u0394acc rewards) over level-based signals whose variance persists. Best practice: deliver process/world-model supervision through an auxiliary loss channel rather than the reward channel (+~20pt with zero measured gradient interference). Monitor a JOINT early-warning signature (entropy decline \u2227 prediction saturation \u2227 length pinning), never entropy alone. Use prediction-saturation shape as a cheap probe of feature difficulty: instant 0.99 = trivially predictable, base-rate plateau = beyond capacity. These prescriptions are scoped to GRPO-family group-relative advantage estimation \u2014 critic-based PPO likely does not share the all-fail-group degeneracy.

## Open Questions / Critiques
All endpoints are single-seed with high validation noise (~\u00b18.4pt at 32 games), so every cross-arm comparison is descriptive, not statistically established; seed replication and a 140-game unified evaluation are only in progress. Group size (n=4) is collinear with the std pathology (small-group \u03c3 estimates are unstable) and a constant-budget group-size ablation is not yet done. The shuffled-gold placebo matching true-gold suggests the auxiliary-loss gain could be a regularization/extra-compute effect rather than genuine world-model information transfer \u2014 the authors admit the in-batch shuffle is leaky (~10% shared golds) and a disjoint-vocabulary placebo is still pending to adjudicate. The collapse-timing law rests on only three scale points and the 8B baseline anomaly (32.8% < 4B) is unexplained. Critic-based estimators (PPO) are deliberately untested. Only two of five HRG coverage rungs and the key \u0394acc progress-reward safety test are still in progress.
